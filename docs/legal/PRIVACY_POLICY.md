# Privacy Policy

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1. Scope
This Privacy Notice ("Notice") outlines how Subcio ("we," "us," or "our") gathers, shares, and utilizes information that identifies you ("Personal Data") when you use our desktop application Subcio ("Application") or engage with us as a customer.

### 1.2. Your Rights
This Notice also details your rights regarding the Personal Data we process about you and how you can exercise those rights.

### 1.3. Privacy Commitment
At Subcio, we are committed to complying with our privacy obligations. This Notice outlines the standards we adhere to for protecting Personal Data, including compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK).

### 1.4. Data Controller
Subcio is the data controller for Personal Data collected via the Application. As a data controller, Subcio ensures that Personal Data processing complies with applicable data protection laws.

### 1.5. Contact
If you have any questions or comments, contact us at: **support@subcio.io**

## 2. Personal Data Collection and Usage

### 2.1. Types of Personal Data and Purposes

The Personal Data we collect and the reasons for its processing include:

| Data Type | Purpose | Legal Basis |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| Email address | License activation, account management | Contractual necessity |
| License key | Software activation and validation | Contractual necessity |
| Device identifier (Machine ID) | License validation, fraud prevention | Legitimate interest |
| Usage statistics (anonymous) | Product improvement, analytics | Legitimate interest |

### 2.2. Local Processing
**Important:** Subcio is a desktop application. All video and audio processing occurs **locally on your device**. We do not upload, store, or process your media files on our servers.

### 2.3. What We Do NOT Collect
- ❌ Video or audio content
- ❌ Subtitle content or transcriptions
- ❌ Personal files on your computer
- ❌ Browsing history
- ❌ Location data (precise)

### 2.4. Automatic Information Collection
During license validation, we may collect:
- IP address (for fraud prevention)
- Device identifier (Machine ID)
- Application version
- Operating system type

## 4. Protecting Your Privacy

We process Personal Data in line with this Notice, ensuring:

- **(a) Fair Processing:** Transparency about how we process Personal Data.
- **(b) Lawfulness:** Processing only on lawful grounds.
- **(c) Purpose Limitation:** Processing for specified, legitimate purposes only.
- **(d) Data Minimization:** Collecting only data that is necessary.
- **(e) Accuracy:** Ensuring Personal Data is accurate and up-to-date.
- **(f) Security:** Using appropriate technical measures to protect data.
- **(g) Retention:** Keeping data only as long as necessary.

---

## 5. Data Storage and Deletion

### 5.1. Retention Period
We retain Personal Data as long as we have a legitimate business need:
- **License data:** Duration of your license validity + 2 years
- **Accounting data:** Up to 10 years (legal requirement)
- **Support communications:** Up to 2 years

### 5.2. Deletion
When there is no longer a legitimate business need, we will delete or anonymize your Personal Data. You may request deletion at any time by contacting support@subcio.io.

---

## 6. Data Security

### 6.1. Security Measures
We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your Personal Data, including:
- Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
- Secure license validation
- No storage of payment card details (handled by LemonSqueezy)

### 6.2. Local Data
All project files, subtitles, and media remain on your local device. We do not have access to these files.

| Right | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Access** | Request a copy of your Personal Data |
| **Rectification** | Request correction of inaccurate data |
| **Erasure** | Request deletion of your data |
| **Restriction** | Request limited processing |
| **Portability** | Receive your data in a portable format |
| **Objection** | Object to certain processing activities |
| **Withdraw Consent** | Withdraw consent at any time |

### 7.2. How to Exercise Rights
To exercise any of these rights, contact us at: **support@subcio.io**

We will respond within 30 days as required by law.

### 7.3. Complaints
If you have concerns about our data practices, you may contact:
- Your local data protection authority
- Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK) if applicable
